PLANETARY LIBERATION CONTRACT

CONTRACT DESIGNATION: VEYRA – OPERATION HOMESTEAD

ISSUING AUTHORITY: Provisional Government of Veyra

CONTRACTING PARTY: Frontier Rapid Enforcement & Elimination Division for Outlaw Mitigation (F.R.E.E.D.O.M.)

CONTRACT CLASSIFICATION: Planetary Liberation / Hostile Occupation

CONTRACT STATUS: ACTIVE

REWARD: Up to 150 Requisition Points (RP)

01 // SITUATION REPORT

To the Commanding Officer of F.R.E.E.D.O.M.,

The Provisional Government of Veyra formally requests the assistance of your organisation in the liberation of our homeworld.

Veyra was once a prosperous frontier colony, supporting a population spread across several major cities and industrial settlements. Its strategic importance lies in its mineral wealth, manufacturing capacity and position along several established trade routes.

This prosperity has been brought to an abrupt end by the Blackthorn Combine, an outlaw military organisation responsible for the seizure of our principal population centres and the collapse of planetary civil authority.

The Combine has established military control over the planet's cities, seized critical infrastructure and imposed its authority upon the civilian population. Our remaining security forces are scattered, poorly equipped and incapable of conducting a coordinated liberation campaign.

The Provisional Government retains limited resources and personnel, but lacks the military strength necessary to reclaim the planet.

We therefore seek to engage F.R.E.E.D.O.M. to conduct a sustained military operation against the Blackthorn Combine and restore Veyra to civilian control.

02 // CONTRACT OBJECTIVES

F.R.E.E.D.O.M. is hereby authorised and contracted to conduct military operations across Veyra with the following primary objectives:

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE – LIBERATION OF CITIES

  • Locate, assault and reclaim every designated city currently occupied by the Blackthorn Combine.
  • Establish secure control over each reclaimed city.
  • Prevent the destruction of civilian population centres and their critical infrastructure wherever operationally possible.
  • Ensure that no more than two designated cities are destroyed during the operation.

SECONDARY OBJECTIVES – OPERATIONAL SUPPORT

The following objectives are not mandatory for contract completion but are considered highly desirable:

  • Destroy or capture Blackthorn Combine military installations.
  • Secure industrial facilities, resource extraction sites and manufacturing infrastructure.
  • Re-establish control over major transport routes and logistical hubs.
  • Recover captured government personnel and military equipment.
  • Protect civilian populations and minimise unnecessary collateral damage.
  • Eliminate or disrupt Blackthorn Combine command and communications capabilities.

Successful completion of secondary objectives may provide additional strategic benefits to the Provisional Government, although no additional requisition payment is guaranteed under this contract.

03 // PAYMENT AND REQUISITION ALLOCATION

Upon successful completion of the primary objectives, F.R.E.E.D.O.M. shall receive a requisition allocation of up to 150 Requisition Points (RP).

The final payment shall be determined by the condition of Veyra's designated cities at the conclusion of the operation.

CONTRACT OUTCOME REQUISITION PAYMENT
All cities reclaimed, none destroyed 150 RP
All remaining cities reclaimed, 1 city destroyed 100 RP
All remaining cities reclaimed, 2 cities destroyed 50 RP
3 or more cities destroyed CONTRACT FAILURE – 0 RP

The destruction of a city constitutes a significant breach of the contract's preservation requirements. Each destroyed city will reduce the final requisition allocation by 50 RP.

The destruction of three or more designated cities shall constitute an automatic failure of the contract, regardless of the military success achieved elsewhere on the planet.

A city is considered destroyed when its population centre has suffered catastrophic damage and can no longer be considered a viable settlement. Temporary occupation, damage to individual structures or the loss of isolated infrastructure does not, by itself, constitute city destruction.

04 // RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

F.R.E.E.D.O.M. is granted operational discretion in the planning and execution of this contract.

The Provisional Government recognises that the liberation of Veyra will require military force and that casualties may be unavoidable. However, the following conditions are expected:

  • Civilian population centres are to be preserved wherever possible.
  • Unnecessary destruction of critical infrastructure is strongly discouraged.
  • Captured civilian personnel are to be treated as non-combatants.
  • Blackthorn Combine military forces are considered hostile combatants.
  • F.R.E.E.D.O.M. may establish forward operating bases, construct defensive positions and secure strategic facilities as required.

The Provisional Government will not interfere with tactical or operational decisions made by F.R.E.E.D.O.M. commanders, provided those decisions remain consistent with the primary contract objectives.

05 // CONTRACT DURATION AND TERMINATION

This contract shall remain active until one of the following conditions is met:

  1. All designated cities have been reclaimed and the operation is declared complete.
  2. Three or more designated cities have been destroyed, resulting in automatic contract failure.
  3. F.R.E.E.D.O.M. formally withdraws from the operation.

Upon completion or termination, the Provisional Government reserves the right to assess the final condition of the planet and determine the requisition payment in accordance with Section 3.

06 // FINAL DECLARATION

Veyra is our home. Its cities are not merely strategic assets, and its population is not an acceptable cost of convenience.

We have watched our government collapse, our security forces scatter and our people live under the authority of criminals and opportunists. We no longer possess the means to liberate ourselves.

We are placing what remains of our world's future in your hands.

We ask that F.R.E.E.D.O.M. do what our own forces could not: break the occupation, reclaim our cities and give Veyra back to its people.

THE PROVISIONAL GOVERNMENT OF VEYRA AWAITS YOUR RESPONSE.
